Language is a hard preference far more often than genre is. A listener who asks for Spanish
music is usually not expressing a genre taste at all, and satisfying the genre while missing
the language is a worse outcome than the reverse. Treat an explicit language request as a
near-filter rather than as one more additive score term.

The catalog covers english, spanish, korean, japanese, and instrumental. "Instrumental" is
also the correct answer to requests phrased as "no lyrics", "no words", or "nothing with
singing", which appear most often in study and sleep contexts.

Popularity-weighted ranking is biased toward English-language catalogs, so language-specific
requests should lower the weight on popularity to avoid burying good non-English matches.
