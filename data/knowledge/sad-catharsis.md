Listeners in low mood frequently seek mood-congruent rather than mood-improving music, and
they report that the congruent choice helps more. A recommender that silently "corrects" a
sad request into an upbeat playlist is overriding a stated preference, so mood match should
be weighted above genre match for these requests.

Target audio features: valence below 0.45, energy 0.3-0.55, moderate-to-high acousticness,
tempo 70-105 BPM. Country, indie rock, slow r&b, and minor-key classical all serve here.

The care boundary matters: mood-congruent recommendation is appropriate for ordinary sadness
and is not a mental-health intervention. A system in this space should not present itself as
support for crisis, and should keep its role as a music picker explicit.
