Wind-down listening works by giving the nervous system a slowly decreasing stimulus. The
useful heuristic is that tempo should sit at or below resting heart rate, roughly 50 to 75
BPM, and that the track should avoid percussive transients entirely.

Target audio features: energy below 0.3, tempo under 75 BPM, acousticness above 0.85, low
danceability. Ambient and solo classical repertoire dominate this space. Valence should be
neutral rather than either euphoric or bleak, since strong emotion in either direction is
activating.

Lyrics, and especially explicit lyrics, work against the goal: language recruits attention
that the listener is trying to release.
