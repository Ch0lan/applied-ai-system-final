Training playlists are the clearest case where a single audio feature dominates: perceived
energy. Listeners entrain their movement cadence to tempo, so tracks in the 120-160 BPM
band map naturally onto running cadence and lifting tempo, and high energy scores
(0.85 and above) correlate with lower rated perceived exertion at the same workload.

Target audio features: energy above 0.85, tempo 120-160 BPM, low acousticness (under 0.15),
high danceability. Genre matters far less than energy here, which is why an EDM track, an
aggressive rock track, and a hard hip-hop track can all sit in the same workout queue.

Explicit content is generally acceptable in a personal training context unless the listener
says otherwise, and aggression in the lyrics is often the point rather than a defect.
