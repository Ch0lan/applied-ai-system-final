Background listening has to survive being ignored. The failure mode is not boredom but
intrusion: a track that suddenly demands attention breaks a conversation. Jazz, bossa,
acoustic soul, and light classical all hold this role because their dynamic range is narrow
and their vocal presence is either absent or conversational.

Target audio features: energy 0.25-0.45, acousticness above 0.6, tempo 80-100 BPM, moderate
valence. Avoid tracks with hard drops or abrupt tempo changes.

Because guests are usually present, explicit lyrics are a poor default in this context even
when the host would not mind them alone.
