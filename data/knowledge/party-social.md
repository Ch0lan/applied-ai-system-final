Social listening is the only context in this corpus where the audience is plural, so the
selection problem is consensus rather than personal fit. High danceability (above 0.75) and
high valence (above 0.7) are the two features that most reliably predict a crowd staying
engaged, and familiarity matters more here than anywhere else, which makes popularity a
legitimate ranking signal rather than a shortcut.

Target audio features: energy 0.75-0.95, danceability above 0.75, valence above 0.7, tempo
110-135 BPM. Genre diversity is a feature, not a bug: a queue that stays inside one genre
for an hour reads as a personal playlist rather than a party.

Because the audience is mixed and not self-selected, explicit content is worth checking with
the host rather than assuming either way.
