Driving is a moderate-attention context: the listener has cognitive room for lyrics but needs
the music to hold a steady arousal level rather than spike it. Mid-to-high energy with low
variance is the target, which is why synthwave, city pop, and mid-tempo rock are commute
staples.

Target audio features: energy 0.6-0.8, tempo 100-125 BPM, low acousticness. Long, repetitive
structures are an advantage rather than a weakness because they do not demand re-orientation
every ninety seconds.

Night driving skews the same profile darker: lower valence, more nostalgia, and a preference
for instrumental or sparse vocal textures.
