Personal listening note, kept separate from the general corpus so it can be swapped per user.

Late-night coding is not the same as daytime studying. The session runs long, so the music
has to stay awake alongside the listener: slightly higher energy than a library session
(around 0.40-0.50 rather than 0.30), and a lower tolerance for very high acousticness, which
reads as sleepy after midnight.

Still instrumental, still no explicit lyrics, but the target is "steady momentum" rather
than "minimum stimulus".
